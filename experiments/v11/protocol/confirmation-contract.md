# v11 finite local confirmation contract

Scope: annotation of an already determined local output, not article truth, editing quality, publication, or general benchmark performance. Ten newly authored synthetic underlying scenarios (5 Korean, 5 English) are fixed before implementation freeze. Six derived mechanical tamper checks do not add underlying cases. There are two normal no-edit controls. New case creation, tuning against hidden outcomes, and reruns after inspecting confirmation results are prohibited within this confirmation batch. Repairs require a separately labeled development follow-up.

The private input schema supplies `case_id`, `language`, `source`, `proposed_patches` (`id`, `before`, `after`, zero-based UTF-8 `start_byte` and exclusive `end_byte`), `review_decisions`, `dependency_components`, `actual_output`, `actual_applied_receipt`, `evidence`, `semantic_facts`, and `authored_free_memo_comparator`. Patches address the original source. Byte replay must match the final local output exactly. A receipt binds source and output SHA-256, actual applied/excluded IDs, individual outcomes, and external-publication state. The test oracle is authored independently of the implementation. Implementers receive this shape only before freeze, never private case text, keys, orders, or exact mutations.

An empty proposal has `allproposed_applied=not_applicable`; it is not a successful repair claim. A partial outcome can have `applied_local=yes` and `allproposed_applied=no`. Unchanged means exact UTF-8 byte identity. Application evidence is not evidence of overall correctness. External publication requires its own evidence; no publication occurs in this local study. Semantic unknowns remain unknown. The presence of unrelated preexisting errors neither proves a newly introduced error nor invalidates an otherwise accurately reported local mutation.

The authored prose comparator is a plausible, newly written review memo, explicitly not a model output. Preserve it without making it artificially incorrect or relabeling it as observed model behavior. If a real native memo generator is available, its immutable raw output and provenance form a separate declared condition; it must not replace the authored comparator or alter the locked scenarios/keys. Missing native generation means that model-comparison claim remains untested.

Primary reader questions are six separate propositions in `reader-rubric.json`. Both conditions show identical source, proposed patches, actual output, and authored evidence; they differ only in the displayed annotation. Do not show answer keys, semantic-fact answer fields, condition labels, intended factor, private order mapping, or other readers' responses. Use opaque packet IDs and fixed randomized order. Keep each proposition's answer and reason; do not collapse them into a single success score. At least two fresh independent readers should receive the same packets. Report disagreements and unknowns without adjudicating them into consensus. Blinding hides condition names, not the visible formatting of prose versus a receipt.

Mechanical confirmation rejects corrupt output, stale receipt, and incomplete write against the completed-output receipt. These checks establish integrity-contract behavior only. No semantic reader score, statistical generalization, human study, or independent world-truth claim follows from them. The stored manifest timestamp records the local lock event; root must record implementation freeze separately and verify temporal order before opening the private cases.
