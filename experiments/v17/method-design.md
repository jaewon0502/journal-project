# v17 method design: bounded source-clause witness ablation

**Proposed design, not an experiment result or frozen preregistration.** The question is whether explicitly checking a source clause's directly linked conditions improves minimal necessary corrections without adding unnecessary edits or new meaning loss. The design was specified from v16 records before inspecting the new evaluation inputs or outputs.

v16's full alignment table showed no observed gain over its baseline on the small controlled sample ([results](../v16/RESULTS.md)). Its later audit found that a locally supported change could broaden a claim while dropping an exception in the same source provision ([audit](../v16/primary-followup-audit.md)). That exposed-case repair is motivation, not a new hidden success. Prior source-first checks, condition preservation, necessity gates and whole-candidate audits are retained. Source anchors/evidence IDs, adjacent-context checks and clause dispositions already occur in v10/v12/v13; those are not new components. This is a **bounded source-witness ablation**, not a new principle, comprehensive source inventory, or evidence of ten independent failed approaches.

| Condition | Input and task | Only process difference |
|---|---|---|
| BASE | Full original, full candidate, fixed reader question, same primary-source packet; necessary exact minimal patches | Existing evidence-based minimal-patch review |
| LINK | Byte-identical article/candidate/question/source packet, same schema and settings | Explicitly check each relied-on clause's directly linked definitions, exceptions, scope and effective dates, confined to relations relevant to that claim |

Both arms have the same evidence-relation witness fields. LINK receives no extra retrieval, facts, alignment table, highlighted answer, evaluator, model call or special schema. BASE may use the same relationships when its ordinary review finds them. The test measures an explicit prompt, not exclusive ability or extra information. Each output uses one fresh native context. Character/call overhead will be measured, without interpreting characters as exact tokens or money.

The intended development stage has two synthetic contexts, with a condition-loss variant and complex correct countercontrol each: four units and eight writer outputs per round. Development may revise the method before freeze and is never evaluation evidence. The intended evaluation has four new full articles (two Korean, two English), each with one researcher-induced source-condition loss and one complex normal countercontrol: eight units, sixteen writer outputs, four correlated article pairs. Full acquired article text, versions and direct primary documents are retained privately. There is no arbitrary article-length limit or compression objective; preserve meaning at its needed length.

A fresh pre-AI reference reads full inputs and is frozen **before evaluation writers run**, without method outputs, condition labels, author expectations or pair mapping. Ambiguity is recorded rather than turned into a fabricated gold label. Necessity requires a concrete change in the answer, scope, attribution or force under the whole context. Helpful detail and value judgments alone do not establish necessity. Complex controls must contain interacting semantic features; length alone does not establish complexity.

Judgment fields distinguish original meaning loss, factual error, condition omission and optional clarification. A supplied but wrong answer is `answer_present_but_incorrect`; `required_answer_missing` means no answer to an atomic question. These two cannot both be yes for that question. Other flags can overlap with separate support: dropping an eligibility exception may create both a factual error and a conclusion-changing omission while leaving an explicit, incorrect answer. A same-detail optional clarification cannot also be a mandatory omission. Field-level AI disagreements and unknowns remain visible.

Patch application uses unique exact candidate spans, nonoverlap and checked dependencies. The post-audit reads the **whole actual applied candidate**, including untouched text, in a fresh condition-blind context. It checks new losses, attribution, criticism, uncertainty and source scope. First outputs and audits are immutable; later corrections or rollbacks are separate follow-up. Local success does not mean the whole article is fact-certified, publication-ready or legally verified. Draft and historical version gaps remain explicit.

The proposed success criterion is at least one additional LINK correct necessary repair, no loss of a BASE correct repair, and no increases in false mandatory claims, unnecessary patches, unjustified local holds or new audited harms among confirmed eligible units. Equal repair performance is no observed repair benefit, not method failure. A perfect BASE result is a ceiling to report, not grounds to make the hidden inputs harder after observing outputs. Extra harm or a lost BASE repair fails the harmless-improvement criterion. Missing outputs and unresolved reference/audit judgments remain unknown and cannot prove harmlessness. Detection-only gains are reported separately from safe repairs.

Report paired article/unit outcomes, detection, delivered repairs, normal-control retention, unnecessary edits/holds, new harm and audit unknowns separately. Do not build a composite political-bias or quality score. The four induced losses are not natural newsroom mistakes; any naturally present inherited error is a separately labeled exploratory finding. Native deployment details, seeds, tokens and cost remain unknown unless the execution interface actually exposes them. A small all-pass result would not establish general completeness, judge reliability or safety.

The private draft contains the shared common instruction, BASE/LINK method instructions, shared JSON schema and proposed preregistration. The execution owner must freeze them after development, together with source/input hashes, independent pre-reference, randomization and application/audit rules. No experiment calls or source acquisition were performed by this design task.
