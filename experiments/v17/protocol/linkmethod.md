# LINK method instruction

Review O, C, S and Q using the common minimal-patch procedure. Assess each candidate claim or proposed correction against the supplied evidence and the full article context, and propose only necessary, supported and safe changes. For each source clause relied on for a claim or correction, explicitly check that clause's directly linked definitions, exceptions, scope and effective-date provisions within the supplied packet; carry a linked condition into the correction only when its omission changes this claim's answer or scope. Bound this check to those direct relations relevant to the claim, without recursive background expansion. If a needed linked provision is absent or ambiguous, record that local evidence gap rather than infer its contents. Record the evidence relations actually relied on in the shared schema.
