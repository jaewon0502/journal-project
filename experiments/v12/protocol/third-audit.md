# Preselected all-output source-first audit

Every real writer output (both conditions on two development and two evaluation cases: eight planned) receives this third audit, regardless of prior graders' pass, failure or disagreement. The auditor does not see their judgments, method label, frozen reference, or other candidate.

First read the complete supplied original article and primary-evidence scope. Save independently generated material questions, source answers and short anchors before opening candidate output. Then inspect candidate and proposed edits. Check exact answer agreement, speaker commitment, condition, temporal relationship, and criticism preservation. Inspect all original paragraphs and explicit cross-paragraph links even where no question was generated. Report audited paragraph IDs and at least actual relation pairs examined; do not claim all relations if only selected pairs were inspected.

Save newly identified harms or material missing conditions with exact evidence and state which survived a no-edit candidate or a prior source-first list. Do not call an original article detail false merely because the primary packet does not verify it. Separate existing unverified assertions, existing omissions, and new candidate harms. A possible concern is not a confirmed defect.

Root compares these independently recorded findings with initial A/B judgments afterwards. A shared blind spot requires a supported material defect both initial graders missed; disagreement alone or a third unsupported concern is not enough. All such proposed defects undergo a separate evidence challenge, with unresolved cases reported as unresolved. No post-hoc relabeling of raw evaluations.
