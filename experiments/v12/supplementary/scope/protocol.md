# Supplementary source-scope diagnostic protocol

This is a small, targeted causal-diagnostic supplement to frozen v12. Original packets, outputs, references, evaluations and scores remain unchanged. These exposed regression diagnostics are not fresh unknown-defect discovery and do not estimate general method performance.

## Design and execution lock

One EN-DEV article; final B-v2 method; two fresh, independent repeats in each source condition, four total. OLD receives exactly the frozen primary evidence. EXPANDED appends complete archived Results (including tables and captions), statistical analyses, and relevant archived step-stratum extended-data captions. Existing article and primary paragraphs and IDs remain byte-for-byte equal as strings and in original order. No new web retrieval. Raw source and all writer inputs stay private.

Recommended order is OLD, EXPANDED, EXPANDED, OLD. Opaque input IDs are independently generated. The same questions, shared instruction, method instruction, output schema and narrative budget are copied exactly from the B-v2 input. The sole experimental manipulation is allowable primary evidence scope, including truthful scope/provenance metadata. More evidence necessarily increases input length; evidence informativeness and length/attention effects cannot be separated by this design. Keep writer environment/model/settings and execution instructions identical and record them at run time. No defect-specific instruction, evaluator labels, reference answers, follow-up analysis or outcomes may enter writer context. Each writer receives only its allocated JSON and identical generic execution instructions. Do not pass the allocation map or this protocol to writers. No prompt adaptation after the lock.

## Provisional measures fixed before supplementary outputs

Evaluate the final article after applying valid exact-anchor patches, and separately record finding/answer coverage. Keep categories rather than combine them into an opportunistic aggregate score. For each run and each dimension, record original span ID, patch IDs, resulting disposition, allowable-source receipt IDs, supported/unsupported/undetermined judgment, and a brief rationale. Distinguish lack of a patch from inaccurate replacement. A mechanically inapplicable patch is recorded separately and receives no assumed benefit.

1. Original low-step range: retained verbatim; retained with attribution/uncertainty qualification; specifically corrected; partially or fully deleted. Record range and associated claim separately. No OLD penalty for not knowing an exact boundary absent from its source. Unsupported invented precision remains a regression in either condition.
2. Low-versus-moderate comparison: retained, qualified, corrected or deleted. Distinguish qualitative ordering from an asserted direct statistical contrast and from a numerical effect difference; require actual allowable evidence for every strengthening.
3. Outcome distinctions: tau accumulation, cognitive change and functional change are separate endpoints. Record conflation, unjustified transfer of effect sizes/significance between endpoints, or deletion of a protected endpoint. Distinguish model predictions, estimated associations and inferential tests.
4. Amyloid condition: record retention/addition/loss or overgeneralization of the applicable baseline amyloid condition, separately from claims about amyloid accumulation itself.
5. Causal status: record observational association versus demonstrated intervention effect; whether randomized confirmation remains appropriately unresolved; modal strengthening or unsupported certainty.
6. Tracker attribution and modal strength: record retention, qualification, strengthening, deattribution or deletion of article-reported tracker claims. Source silence does not refute an attributed possible claim; qualification may preserve an unverified article-reported claim without certifying it.

## Two distinct evaluation panels

Common protected-original propositions: compare preservation, justified correction and collateral loss of article meaning across both conditions. Attribute corrections only to receipts actually available in that condition. An unresolved claim can be retained as article-reported with its original uncertainty; absence from OLD does not make a claim false. Report both broad preservation and whether added strength is warranted.

Extra-evidence capability: separately evaluate exact category endpoints, outcome-specific comparisons, reference-category interpretation, statistical significance and model-prediction details that EXPANDED enables. OLD is marked unavailable/not assessable where those details are absent; do not add its unknowns to an error denominator. Newly enabled detail is not automatically required in a minimum patch. Judge any added detail for source-supportedness; report useful correction/qualification separately from omission.

Allowable evidence is each allocated packet, not the complete archive or retrospective follow-up. Evaluators may consult the private manifest to verify packet membership. Do not infer a direct pairwise significance test from two comparisons to a common reference, infer a numerical tau percentage from standardized coefficients, or equate nonsignificance with proof of no association.

## Interpretation and reporting

Report the four individual outputs with the predeclared dimensions and counts by condition (denominators at most two). Separate common preservation from extra-evidence capability. Describe consistency and discrepancies; do not claim statistical identification or generalization from four runs. Scope-associated changes are provisional diagnostic evidence, subject to run variation and the inherent length confound. No retroactive v12 rescoring, new aggregate success definition, method tuning or further prompt adaptation. Preserve raw outputs privately and publish quote-free summary metadata only. This preparation does not launch writers.
