# Supplementary known-issue transfer diagnostic

This final bounded supplement tests transfer of two explicitly supplied, already exposed source facts into an edited EN-EVAL article. It is a known-issue transfer regression, not fresh defect discovery, not held-out evaluation, and not a replacement for any original v12 scores. The starting article is exactly the displayed B candidate in the privately retained reviewer packet ending 094e3. That candidate previously recognized the facts in question answers without incorporating them into article text. The starting candidate is deliberately reused. No prior writer JSON, prior question answers, other candidate, or grader verdict is supplied to writers.

## Frozen design

Four fresh independent writers: two ordinary-sentence fact presentations (F), two typed-row presentations (T), in execution order F, T, T, F. The four inputs have opaque independent IDs. Only the representation of the same two supplied facts varies: F presents ordinary concise source-linked sentences; T presents speaker, time, measure, comparison, modality, and source fields. T adds no substantive fact. The private equivalence record maps every field to its sentence counterpart. Typed field labels and layout necessarily change presentation and length; this experiment does not separate all possible attention effects within that presentation change.

All writers receive the exact same starting article, complete eligible primary evidence, three questions, local-edit instruction, output schema, English 900-word narrative cap and generic execution instruction. The schema is reused unchanged from v12 and contains findings, exact-anchor patches, question answers and limitations. Budget exclusions remain JSON keys/IDs and copied exact anchors. Apart from the opaque run identifier, repair-goal representation is the sole packet difference. The eligible source remains only the frozen Monetary Policy Summary section; archived meeting minutes, reports and other documents remain ineligible. No web, authorization flow, new cases, source expansion or prompt adaptation is permitted.

The shared editor instruction explicitly requires both repairs in the final article while restricting changes to necessary local patches. It preserves surrounding criticism, counterarguments, existing meaning, time relationships, forecast attribution and uncertainty. Each replacement must use a unique exact anchor in the unmodified starting article; patches must not overlap. Source silence does not justify deleting an attributed claim. Each fresh writer receives only its allocated JSON plus the identical private generic execution instruction, with no sibling outputs, allocation map, this protocol, grader reference, or prior grader verdicts. Keep model, environment, reasoning settings and execution instructions identical across all four runs and record them before launch. This preparation launches no writers.

## Targets fixed before outputs

The two targets are (1) the observed recent rise in measures of household inflation expectations and (2) the likely smaller negative UK growth AND inflation impacts of trade uncertainty and new tariff announcements relative to global impacts. Their semantic coverage requirements, exact source receipts and component checks are frozen in a private grader-only reference. There is no expected patch wording and no new target discovery. Presence in findings or answers alone does not satisfy either target.

For each run, apply mechanically valid exact-anchor patches once to reconstruct the final article. Retain raw output, patches and the applied article privately. Record invalid anchors, overlapping edits and application failures separately; never silently repair an output or assume a benefit for an inapplicable patch. Grade actual final-article presence as covered, partial, absent or unknown and source support as supported, partial, unsupported or unknown, with exact article and eligible-source receipts. Report each target independently and supported full target coverage out of two. Report finding mention and question-answer mention separately, without using them as substitutes for article coverage.

Independently assess preservation or justified correction of original meaning; criticism and counterarguments; time relationships and forecast attribution; and observed-versus-hypothetical and likely-versus-certain modality. Inspect every changed clause and the relations it creates with nearby unchanged text. Record all newly introduced harms, including those outside the two targets, with exact anchors and evidence. Separate demonstrated harms, partial coverage and unresolved issues; do not expand the frozen target denominator retrospectively. Source silence is not evidence of falsity. Do not conflate inflation expectations with actual CPI, growth with inflation, global with UK impact, or a likelihood judgment with certainty.

## Reporting limits and stop rule

Report all four individual outcomes and descriptive condition counts with denominators of two runs per condition. If both F and T succeed, there is no evidence here that T is better than F. If they differ, describe a provisional representation-associated diagnostic result with run variation and the tiny sample acknowledged; do not claim general performance or statistical identification. Both conditions give the repair goals extra attention relative to prior runs, and that intervention is not isolated from the prior runs. Therefore any improvement over the earlier candidate cannot be attributed specifically to typed representation.
