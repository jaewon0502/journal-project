## Actual fixed P19 matters

I inspected `local-workspace/journal-v26-private/selection/input.json` (P19). It changes more than produced→estimated: “in the experiment the magenta cells” becomes “Rayudu says the experimental system”; the production verb becomes an estimation construction; “conventional solar panels of similar size” becomes “the conventional solar reference.” These must not be reduced in reporting to two independent word edits. In particular, the inserted attribution can scope over the numeric comparison as well as the estimate, and a system/cells referent shift may alter the claim. Restoring a similar-size phrase does not by itself validate the whole attribution or comparison. The supplied source packet, not intuition about plausibility, determines support.

## Narrow question and minimum fair design

1. Freeze the same full original article, source packet, actual whole P19 before/after, selector policy, schema and audit criteria before any new outputs. W exposes the actual proposal as one selectable unit; T deterministically derives token-diff edit atoms; S has a fresh-context model derive exact semantic subpatches from that same proposal. Do not supply the historical P19 diagnosis, targeted repair wording, author rationale or expected accepted unit list to decomposer/selectors/auditors. This remains an exposed one-event regression for the research team, not a holdout.
2. Freeze T's tokenizer, Unicode/whitespace handling, diff algorithm/tie-breaking, and insertion ordering before running. Preserve all spaces and punctuation; do not silently normalize. For both T and S require nonoverlapping original-coordinate edits, exact before spans, allowed replacement provenance, and **all units applied = the exact fixed whole proposed after-text**. No missing changed spans, new wording, overlap or silent duplicate insertion. Empty-span insertions require offsets and a deterministic same-boundary rule; the older nonempty-anchor engines cannot be imported unchanged.
3. “Exact recomposition” is necessary but does not certify that every possible subset is a faithful subpatch. S can relocate boundaries across equal text or introduce canceling edits that yield new subset wording while still recomposing exactly. Restrict S to a partition/grouping of canonical edit operations, or specify a stricter original/target monotonic alignment contract that rules out canceled edits and novel replacement bytes. If semantic split requires a different alignment, record this as a representation difference and mechanically verify it rather than quietly accepting arbitrary rewrites. Semantic grouping of T atoms is the cleanest minimal contract, but it cannot split an indivisible T replacement; disclose that limitation.
4. All three selectors get full O, full proposed C, same S, same questions, same actual candidate-rendering access, same decision fields and conservative evidence/necessity/preservation/context-safety rules. The selectable unit list is the treatment. Use the same fresh-context selector settings and number of selection passes. S's separate decomposition call and semantic judgment are additional work: count them and frame S−T as **semantic decomposition workflow**, not a pure cost-matched boundary effect or more capable model. With one pass per arm, stochastic variability is unmeasured.
5. Decomposition should output spans/groups only, without risk labels or recommended accept/reject decisions visible to selection. Otherwise S adds an extra source-informed reviewer and decision cues. If dependencies are elicited during decomposition, suppress their conclusions from selection or provide the same dependency assessment stage to every arm, and report the changed intervention honestly.
6. Generate real subset candidates before dependency judgments. Do not infer semantic independence from nonoverlapping spans or token atoms. Use one shared conservative dependency policy: evidence for mandatory direction, joint cycles, unknown relationship holds; final whole-text audit remains required even with complete pairwise coverage. If not implementing v15's full two-review all-pairs contract, call the v27 policy an adapted check rather than a validated replay of v15. Budget/denominator grows with unit count and is itself a granularity cost.
7. Apply only actual selector decisions with the frozen renderer. Blind final auditors to arm labels, decomposition rationale, historical failures and expected result; give full O, actual final text and S. Deduplicate identical final texts with an explicit mapping. Audit all changed AND retained meaning: estimate status, numeric quantities/units/time, comparison qualifier, referent identity, attribution scope, criticism, remaining original problems, and combination safety. These criteria belong in the auditor's common general schema; avoid a P19-specific clue that makes detection appear autonomous. Preserve pre-audit selection and post-audit delivery/hold separately; no automatic repair loop.
8. Predefine per-article, source-anchored outcomes rather than success per selected atom: required correction delivered; unsupported qualifier loss; unsupported attribution/referent expansion; new harm/unknown; safe correction unnecessarily held; technical invalidity. All-reject is safe retention but missed correction, never overall success. Different atom counts invalidate per-atom acceptance-rate comparisons. Report one historical article/event, three selector outputs, S decomposition call(s), number of actual candidate texts/audits, not three independent events or approaches.

## Interpretation boundary

W vs T tests whether making smaller literal edit units selectable helps on this particular bundled failure; W vs S tests the entire semantic decomposition-plus-selection workflow; T vs S can identify an observed difference between those workflows but cannot isolate semantic boundary quality from extra model work, grouping cardinality or prompt content. If all three achieve the same final delivery, report no observed granularity benefit. If S/T retain the necessary correction and avoid the loss only after the auditor explicitly names the failure, label it prompted repair. A final safe candidate would demonstrate this bounded exposed-case repair, not generalized autonomous minimal editing or complete dependency detection. No additional independent-family success/failure count follows.
