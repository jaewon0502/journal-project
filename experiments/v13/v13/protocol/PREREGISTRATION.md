Question: can supported local corrections be delivered while material unresolved edits are held, without introducing loss through adjacency or dependencies?

Scope: six new authored fictional source/article/patch bundles and two exposed v12 real-output bundles. Synthetic cases are not actual news or six independent real-world events. Real repeats are regression only. Intended controls: normal/no-edit, criticism preservation, unrelated evidence gaps, unresolved material conditions, mixed safe/risky edits, interaction/dependency. Freeze all source/article/proposed patch bytes and questions before AI review; author expectations separate and not shown to reviewers or delivery auditors. An independent validator reads source+article+proposals before author expectations and records ambiguity. One pass, no prompt adaptation after results. Ambiguous examples stay reported rather than relabeled to improve success.

Two independent fresh-context risk reviews per bundle. Same source, original, all proposals and fixed questions; no gate names, author labels, previous scores. Reviews identify necessary supported corrections and distinguish stylistic preference from actor/attribution/strength/condition/negation/number/time/comparison/causality/responsibility/criticism changes. Review source scope and full original including adjacent relations. Evidence absence is not refutation. Unrelated original evidence gaps are not new harms. Each patch has five enums (yes/no/unknown): warranted, evidence_support, preserves_untargeted_meaning, context_safe, application_valid. Dependencies list patch IDs; possible unlocalized material risks are global. Each background issue is material yes/no/unknown and affects_patch_ids; empty IDs means unrelated only if affirmative explanation supports this classification. Preference-only differences are recorded separately and do not count as semantic risk. Every judgment has anchors/reasons, confidence qualitative.

G0 strict bundle rule: with complete/hash-bound reviews, all patch judgments must be unanimously yes; no unknown/no/disagreement in the five fields anywhere, no unresolved background issue or material global issue. Otherwise deliver original. This is a newly harmonized whole-bundle counterpart, not bit-for-bit v12 release_gate replay. v12 original10holds retained separately.
G1 selective rule: require same completeness/hash binding; accept only patches unanimously yes on all five fields, with no mapped material/unknown issue, and closed dependencies. Unlocalized material/unknown global risk holds entire bundle. Differences that are positively classified as preference-only, or documented unrelated original-scope gaps, may remain. Unknown patch relation or dependency never treated as safe. Missing/invalid fields hold. Apply accepted patches simultaneously to original with unique nonoverlapping anchors. Recompute dependency closure; no partial clause rewrite or AI-generated new patch at gate stage.

Both proposed deliveries receive independent fresh-context final source/original/delivery audits with gate/method labels hidden. No author labels or initial reviews. Audit changed and retained meaning, adjacent relationships, criticism, time, source support, any missing requested correction; unchanged original errors separated from introduced errors. Do not use disagreement-free acceptance as truth. If any material introduced risk or unknown safety remains, final deliverable is held/rolled back; record pre-audit release separately from final release. Shared identical delivered texts may be audited once with mappings, not falsely count duplicate judgments as independent. No automatic second repair cycle.
