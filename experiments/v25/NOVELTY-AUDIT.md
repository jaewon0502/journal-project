## What would distinguish the proposed intervention

1. Preserve a genuinely external record as acquired: publisher/registry key, typed metric definition, entity/arm/plot key, time/population/treatment, value/unit, version and provenance. Freeze it before article-driven mapping or scoring. If the experiment authors manufacture the row, call it a synthetic externally supplied fixture; it cannot establish real-world external authority.
2. Separately expose and assess the article-mention → published entity-key decision and entity-key → applicable metric-row decision. Equal keys do not solve an incorrectly chosen article entity or metric. An article without sufficient identity must remain locally unresolved; successful arithmetic is not identity evidence.
3. Be precise about the contrast. A registry-enriched arm versus a prose-only arm changes information and possibly retrieval/format/compute together. To isolate use of keys, both arms can receive the same acquired evidence with the key relation selectively available under a predeclared design; to test practical augmentation, report the compound difference honestly. No performance result is supplied by this audit.
4. Include same-entity wrong-number correction and different-entity legitimate-value preservation when a future test is authorized. Merely rejecting every unresolved case does not demonstrate useful resolution. Fabricated IDs and irrelevant but real anchors are especially relevant to the old gate's limitation.

This supports a **candidate new information intervention**, not an already proven independent approach family. A typed row compiled by the same AI from the same evidence remains A03/binding-ledger lineage; a schema checker remains the v24 enforcement lineage. A real independent registry may change the evidence source without by itself establishing a new algorithmic family. Freeze the exact mechanism and its relation to existing families before execution; do not award a family simply for a new name.

## Trained cross-document coreference/relation alignment

A separately trained resolver that predicts whether mentions across documents refer to the same entity, or a trained relation aligner that predicts correspondence between article predicates and source metrics, is mechanistically different from SequenceMatcher and from asking an additional general AI auditor. Such learned decisions still require measurement; they are not authoritative identity and may share correlated errors. External ID linkage, learned mention coreference, and metric/relation alignment are three separable operations, not interchangeable labels.

No usable checkpoint, inference setup, training corpus, evaluation split, or actual cost estimate for such a resolver was established by the inspected records. Obtaining/validating a compatible model, labels, dependencies and language/domain coverage adds setup and compute cost; this audit did not inventory all installed models or attempt installation. Therefore call this option **unexecuted and availability/cost unverified**, not failed, impossible, free, or a tenth independent approach. No numeric unavailable-model cost can responsibly be quoted from these files.

Additional bounded request from parent: evaluate a same-editor contrast that adds the actual publisher-rendered table page alongside identical full source text, preserving native row/column/section relations rather than asking AI to invent a binding ledger. No new trial performed.

The candidate's defensible distinction is **a controlled manipulation of access to publisher-native row/column/layout relationships at the binding step, with the full text and editor instruction held fixed**. It still requires fallible visual/semantic interpretation and sufficient resolution.

A full page may add unrelated text, repeated evidence, visual modality, attention cues and compute along with geometry. Preserve identical substantive source coverage where possible; report the contrast as **adding the native table-page view**, not a perfectly isolated pure-layout effect. Avoid AI-added highlighting, selective answer-bearing crops, or hand-reconstructed tables unless these are explicitly separate interventions. Do not call gains evidence of solved coreference or a new independent family merely because the table view is different.
