Minimal **CANDIDATE** replacements, not applied:

1. Reviewed-text sentence: “Text generated or manipulated by AI and published to inform the public on matters of public interest needs to be disclosed as such, unless it has undergone human review or editorial control and a person or organisation holds editorial responsibility for publication.” This only repairs the reviewed-text branch; it is not an exhaustive description of other exceptions.
2. Open-source sentence: “Open-source general-purpose models are not exempt from the copyright-policy or training-content-summary requirements.”
3. The 36-month deadline applies to the high-risk systems covered by Article 6(1), rather than all high-risk systems.” This is a summary, not exhaustive transitional guidance.
