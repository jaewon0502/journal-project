# v16 source–original conflict controls: preregistration

This small synthetic falsification asks one mechanism question: **When stipulated primary evidence contradicts O, does the change-location table induce restoration of O instead of a source-supported decision?** The source-over-original rule already exists in v16; this is a targeted control of its execution, not a new semantic principle.

Exactly four O/C/S/Q comparison units represent two independent fictional news contexts (one Korean, one English), with two candidate variants per context: two pairs, not four independent events. Each original has one actual error under explicitly stipulated fictional primary records. Within each pair only one candidate slot differs. One candidate is a justified correction; the other supplies a different unsupported value. Unchanged context retains time limits, attributed criticism and response, uncertainty, denominators or conditions. These are short complete synthetic articles, not real full-article evidence or independent verification of real-world accuracy.

Each unit uses the existing neutral v16 fields: unit_id, article_id, original_text, candidate_text, sources, fixed_questions, read_scope. No expected labels or pair keys occur in writer inputs. The exact same frozen common-instruction-v1.md is used. Its conservative sentence about holding when restoration conflicts with sources remains unchanged: a hold is recorded separately from unsafe restoration and from a successful source-supported repair. The desired harmful-case endpoint is a safe repair to the explicitly evidenced alternative; restoring O is never scored as correct.

M0 receives the unit alone; M1 receives the identical unit plus the existing deterministic full alignment artifact. Four units × two methods gives eight planned writer calls, each in an isolated context with one candidate. Model, reasoning budget, output schema and role must match between methods. The dispatcher alone sees the task list; writers receive only the common instruction and assigned input, with no sibling candidate, hypothesis, review, or output. No writers have been executed by preparation. Any independent input reviewer must be blind to hypotheses and writer outputs and must freeze adjudication before writers run. If that review is not performed, report author-hypothesis scoring explicitly and do not call it independent validation.

## Fixed scoring axes

Score each unit and method separately; summarize paired outcomes by the two underlying contexts. Keep raw outputs immutable and distinguish JSON/schema failure, invalid anchor, and unexecuted calls from semantic failure. Unknowns and holds remain visible; do not silently remove them from the four-unit denominator.

1. **Candidate factual diagnosis:** identify the exact source conflict on harmful candidates; do not invent a conflict for the justified correction. Original meaning loss and factual error are independent axes; acknowledging justified loss is allowed.
2. **Local action:** justified correction requires preservation_decision=no_change, no patches, and no local hold. Harmful candidate requires preservation_decision=repair with a necessary patch to the stipulated evidence-supported value. Hold is abstention and an unsuccessful endpoint here, not restoration and not detection success by itself.
3. **Repair target:** apply exact unique candidate anchors mechanically. Score source-supported corrected proposition, blind restoration of the original erroneous proposition, another unsupported proposition, or no valid repair separately. Semantically equivalent minimal spans may pass; exact span size is not the semantic gold.
4. **Collateral preservation:** retain every other time, attribution, criticism, response, uncertainty, eligibility condition and denominator. A correct target plus collateral change fails minimal repair safety. On justified controls, any patch fails no-change maintenance.
5. **Contract and scope:** patch findings must have necessity, evidence_support, preserves_other_meaning and context_safe=yes; no overlapping patches; no unsupported factual or omission finding. Overall readiness and unrelated coverage are separate and do not alone fail local correctness. Report unsupported full verification claims separately.

Primary per-method endpoint is joint success on both variants of each context (0–2 pairs): justified no_change plus source-supported harmful repair without collateral change. Also report correction no-change maintenance (0–2), harmful exact detection (0–2), harmful safe repair (0–2), unnecessary local hold (0–4), blind restoration (0–4), and technical invalidity (0–4), always as counts. Applied-patch safety has NA denominator if there are no patches. A source-supported repair with poor evidence explanation is distinguished from full contract success.

Any blind restoration falsifies reliable application of the existing source-priority rule on this fixture. Any additional M1 false positive, unnecessary hold, blind restoration, or collateral damage contradicts harmless improvement on these controls. Equal outcomes mean no observed alignment benefit. Four passes cannot establish generalization, superiority or complete error detection. No thresholds, reference truth or questions may be revised after writer exposure; changes require a dated deviation and separate rerun.
